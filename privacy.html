WordSplit Privacy Policy

WordSplit collects no data. Your scores, settings, achievements and challenge history live only on your device.

If you sign in to Game Center, Apple handles leaderboards and achievements under Apple's privacy policy. WordSplit never sees your Apple account.

Challenge links you share contain only what you choose to send: the puzzle words, your chosen display name and your score. They go directly to whoever you send them to — never to us.

No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no accounts. Questions? hello@wordsplit.app
